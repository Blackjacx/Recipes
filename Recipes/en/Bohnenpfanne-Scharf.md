# Spicy Bean Skillet à la Bud Spencer & Terence Hill

## Ingredients for 4 servings
    300 g           seasoned minced pork (Mett)
    2 tins          kidney beans (400 g each)
    1 tin           white beans (400 g)
    1 tin           chickpeas (400 g)
    200 g           sweetcorn
    2 large         onions
    5               garlic cloves
    1               red pepper
    1               chilli (as brave as you are)
    2 tbsp          tomato purée
    400 g           chopped tomatoes (tinned)
    400 ml          beef stock
    2 tbsp          olive oil
    1 tsp           cumin
    1 tsp           smoked paprika
    1 tsp           salt
                    pepper

## Preparation
1. Finely chop the onions, garlic, pepper and chilli.
1. Heat the oil in a large pan and fry the minced pork until crumbly. Add the onions, garlic and chilli and fry until the onions are translucent.
1. Add the pepper and fry for another 2 minutes. Stir in the tomato purée and toast it briefly.
1. Deglaze with the beef stock, add the tinned tomatoes and stir everything well.
1. Drain and rinse the beans and chickpeas and add them to the pan together with the drained sweetcorn. Season with cumin, paprika, salt and pepper.
1. Leave everything to simmer for 15-20 minutes, stirring now and then.

## Tip
Control the heat with the amount of chilli – from mild to "Bud Power". Serve with crusty bread, polenta or rice. The bean skillet tastes even better the next day – just like a good spaghetti western.

## Duration
approx. 35 min
