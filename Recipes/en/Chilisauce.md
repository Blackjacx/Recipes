# Chilli Sauce

## Ingredients
    2 tbsp          coconut oil
    1 tsp           sesame oil
    2 tbsp          chilli flakes
    1/4             onion
    2               garlic cloves
    1 tsp           brown sugar
    1 tbsp          sesame seeds
    3-4 tbsp        soy sauce
    1 tbsp          rice vinegar
    1 dash          fish sauce

## Preparation
1. Heat the coconut oil and sesame oil in a small pan.
1. Finely chop the onion and garlic and mix them in a heatproof bowl with the chilli flakes, brown sugar and sesame seeds.
1. Pour the hot oil over the mixture and leave to cool.
1. Stir in the soy sauce, fish sauce and rice vinegar.

## Tip
Goes well with [cucumber salad](Gurkensalat-Chinesisch.md) and noodles.

## Duration
approx. 15 min
