# Pulled Beef

## Ingredients
    1               beef shank slice
    2 tbsp          mustard
    3 tbsp          soy sauce
    2 tsp           cumin
    2 tsp           sweet paprika
    500 ml          beer
                    salt, pepper

## Preparation
1. Knead the shank slice well with the mustard, soy sauce, cumin, paprika, salt, pepper and 250 ml of the beer.
1. Leave to marinate overnight, approx. 12 h.
1. Top up with the remaining 250 ml of beer and put it in the oven at 120 °C for 2 h.
1. Take the meat off the bone and pull it apart.

## Tip
Perfect for burgers.

## Duration
approx. 12 h marinating<br />
2 h in the oven
